# Privacy Policy for TestDummy

TestDummy is a Chrome extension designed to autofill forms on local development environments and test websites.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

All functionality is executed entirely within your browser. No data is sent to external servers, and no user information is shared with third parties.

## What We Do Not Collect or Track

- Personally identifiable information
- Web browsing history
- User activity
- Location
- Authentication or financial data

## How TestDummy Works

TestDummy operates entirely locally within your browser:
- Test profile data is stored only in your browser's local storage
- Form autofill happens directly in your browser without any network requests
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry is collected
